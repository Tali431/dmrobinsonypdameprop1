Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the YPD?

Just come through during the Sunday Service at 09:00 AM! Anyone from the ages of 0 years to 26 years of age is welcome. You can also fill this form and we will contact you shortly.


Do I need to be a member of Darie Mae Robinson Church?

Yes, you do need to be a church member first to join the DMR YPD, because we are a youth ministry of the church. However, if you are not a member yet, you can still come and join us for our events and worship.


What is the age limit to join the YPD?>

The age limit to joining the YPD is 26. However there is still other auxililaries available for different age groups, such as the Womens Missionary Society(from 18 years of age), Richard Allen Young Adult Council(from the ages of 26-35),Sons of allen(from the ages of 18),Charlottle Maxeke Ladies Fellowship(from the age of 18),Lay Organisation(from the age of 18) and the ACE League(from the age of 2)


What happens at YPD Meetings?

At YPD meetings, we have fellowship time, worship, and discussions about the organisations and the duties we have tp carry out within it as the members, according to the constituion and bylaws we have.


How much do camps and retreats cost?

The cost varies depending on the location and duration of the camp or retreat. We announce costs on the Events page and WhatsApp group. We also do fundraising to help everyone attend.
